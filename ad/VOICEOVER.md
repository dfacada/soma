# Dayhub ad: the voiceover

Every word of the voiceover is already on screen, so the ad works with the sound off (most people watch it that way),
and if you record it, the text on screen doubles as its captions. Record it yourself or hand this page to a voice actor.

## Two voices

- **The member.** Someone on an evening walk, saying their day into their phone the way they'd leave a voice note:
  casual, a little breath, a half smile, nothing performed. Record it outside if you can (a quiet street, phone held at
  chest height, as in the ad), or in a room with the phone a hand's length away.
- **The narrator.** Warm, close and unhurried, like a friend telling you about something good. No announcer voice, no
  hype. A small room with soft things in it (a wardrobe full of clothes is ideal), the mic or phone 15 to 20 cm away,
  slightly off to the side so the p's don't pop.

One person can do both. Keep the member's line looser and the narrator's tighter.

## Recording and laying it in

- Record each line on its own, three or four takes, as 48 kHz WAV (any voice-memo app that records uncompressed is fine).
- If a good take runs a little long, speed it up by up to 5% in the editor rather than rushing the read.
- Lay it over the finished ad and turn the music down by about 6 dB under the voice. In CapCut: select the ad's clip,
  lower its volume to about 50% where the voice speaks, or use keyframes on the volume.
- Keep the words exactly as written. Each one was checked against what the app does today; if you change a line, the
  text on screen has to change with it (`ad/ad.js`), and it still has to be true.
- Using an AI voice instead? Choose a stock voice whose licence allows ads, never a copy of a real person's voice, and
  turn on the platform's AI label when you post (see `PRODUCTION.md`).
