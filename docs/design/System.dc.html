Rules
Spacing. 16 px page gutter, 12 px between cards, 8 px between chips. Touch targets 44 px or taller; habit chips are the one 32 px exception because they sit inside a card, never alone.
Hierarchy. Each screen has one display-size number or date. Domain color goes on at most three things per card: the dot, the fill, one number.
Privacy is visible. The lock icon and the word "Encrypted" appear wherever journal text is shown. Insights say in one sentence what leaves the phone.
